Review Queue

ELIGIBLE coins whose current rolling-24h VMC is at least 1.5× at least one of its own 3-day, 7-day or 30-day means of completed UTC daily ratios. Ordered by 3d, then 7d, then 30d result (YES before NO before N/A), then the 3-day multiple. A list for human chart review — not a buy signal.

All Eligible

Every coin that passes all hard filters (rank 101–1200, market cap ≥ $15M, listed ≥ 60 days, not a stablecoin or tokenized asset, an LBank Spot pair) — including 0-star rows and failed windows.

Needs Review

Missing or ambiguous inputs are never silently eligible: rank, cap or listing date missing, an ambiguous class, an ambiguous LBank ticker (or an unverified pair when manual verification is on), or no usable LBank list. VMC windows are still computed so you can decide what to resolve first. Open a coin to confirm or reject its LBank mapping.

Excluded

Coins with at least one known failed hard filter, with every reason. Windows are not evaluated for them (no daily history is requested).
